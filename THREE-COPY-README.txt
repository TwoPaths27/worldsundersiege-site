STARTER DECK THREE-COPY UPDATE

CHANGES
- Each Starter Deck may now be purchased up to three times.
- Stock begins at "3 remaining".
- The count decreases after every purchase.
- At zero remaining, the deck becomes gray and displays SOLD OUT.
- Local and Supabase purchase counts are merged.
- Existing array-based ownership data is automatically treated as one purchase.
- Each purchase still costs 1,000 Gold.
- Extra card copies continue converting into Gold according to the existing rules.
