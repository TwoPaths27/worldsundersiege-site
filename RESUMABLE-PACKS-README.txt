Worlds Under Siege — Resumable Pack Openings

This build saves paid pack and booster-box purchases inside the same local player profile as Gold and collection ownership.

Behavior:
- Gold and the unopened purchase are saved together immediately.
- Pack contents are generated and saved before the reveal animation begins.
- Reloading or returning later resumes the exact same pack without charging Gold again.
- Booster boxes remember opened packs, remaining packs, pulls, duplicate Gold, and summary totals.
- Collection rewards are applied together with the saved opening update, preventing the same pack from being awarded twice.
- A completed single pack is removed from pending purchases automatically.
- A completed booster box remains available at its summary until the player returns or buys another box.

Replace the corresponding files in the GitHub Pages site with this build.
