Worlds Under Siege - Mobile Header and Pack Sounds Update

Replace your existing open-packs.html, open-packs.css, and open-packs.js with the included files.
Copy the included sounds folder into the same directory.

Sound behavior:
- pack-rip.mp3 plays when a booster pack is clicked.
- pack-pop.mp3 plays for every pack flash during Open All Remaining Packs.
- super-rare.mp3 plays when a Super Rare is revealed.
- ultra-rare.mp3 plays when an Ultra Rare is revealed.
- secret-rare-1.mp3 and secret-rare-2.mp3 play together when a Secret Rare is revealed.
