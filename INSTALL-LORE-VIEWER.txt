Worlds Under Siege lore viewer update

Replace/add these files in the repository root:
- cards.html
- lore.js

Keep your current cards.js.

In GitHub Desktop:
1. Copy cards.html and lore.js beside index.html and cards.js.
2. Commit to main.
3. Push origin.
4. Wait for the Pages deployment and refresh the Cards page.

Adding history:
Open lore.js and add entries inside the lore object:

"BOA-001": `First paragraph.

Second paragraph.`,

Cards without an entry remain blank.

The popup layout:
- Card image(s) on the left
- Card name, number, type, and set on the right
- Scrollable History & Origins section
- Dr Jekyl / Mr Hyde shows both images
- Mobile view stacks the image above the history
