GLOBAL CLICK SOUND — PACK/REVEAL EXCLUSIONS

The generic mouse-click.mp3 no longer plays when:
- selecting/opening a booster pack,
- selecting a pack from a booster box,
- revealing individual pack cards,
- using Reveal All,
- revealing Starter Deck cards,
- using Starter Deck Reveal All.

Those interactions keep their own existing sound effects.

All other buttons, links, menus, settings, and navigation still use
sounds/mouse-click.mp3.
