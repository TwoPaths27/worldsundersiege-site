# Worlds Under Siege v18.7.2 — Recruitment and Popover Hotfix

## Fixed

- Fixed recruitment failing after its animations with `enterPermanent() requires a permanent card`.
- `createUnit()` now preserves normalized card type, traits, capabilities, gameplay identity, and uniqueness data.
- Newly recruited Characters, Armies, Animals, and generic Units are recognized as permanents by the authoritative entry gate.
- Allowed the left sidebar to show overflow so the Advanced Stops panel can extend toward the battlefield.
- Widened and shifted the Advanced Stops panel slightly to the right.
