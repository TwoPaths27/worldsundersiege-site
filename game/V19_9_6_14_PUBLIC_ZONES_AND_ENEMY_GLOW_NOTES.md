# V19.9.6.14 — Public Zones and Enemy Glow

- Discard and Banish arrays are now authoritative for destroyed Units, destroyed Items, and resolved Actions.
- Zone counts come from the actual arrays.
- Clicking Discard or Banish shows the cards in the public-zone browser.
- The newest card is displayed face-up on the pile with a grayscale treatment.
- Enemy units now use a red ownership glow instead of gold.
