# V19.9.6.5 — Interaction Lock and Action Discard

- Locks gameplay input for the full Action resolution presentation.
- Keeps cards, units, movement, attacks, recruitment, and End Turn unavailable while resolving.
- Animates the resolving Action from the stack to its owner's Discard zone.
- Restores control only after the discard animation finishes and the stack is empty.
- Preserves Counter windows before resolution.
