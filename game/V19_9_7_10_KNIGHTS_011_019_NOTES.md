# V19.9.7.10 — Knights 011–019

Implemented the first full gameplay pass for BOA-011 through BOA-019, plus Conceal and enemy Item presentation fixes.

- Removed the large question mark from concealed battlefield cards.
- Enemy equipped spaces retain the blue Item underglow while preserving the red enemy border/glow.
- Lady of the Lake: themed Deck search for an Item, move to hand, then shuffle.
- Queen Guinevere: friendly Knights gain +1 RNG and +1 SPD.
- Sir Mordred: reveal during controller's turn may temporarily take an opposing Character in range; control restores before the next turn, attached Items follow the Character's controller, and mounted stolen Characters are separated before return.
- Sir Percival: board-selection prompt heals one friendly Unit fully.
- Sir Bors: gains +1 ATK for each Item controlled and receives extra Unit/Construct attacks based on Items equipped; Stronghold attacks remain once per turn.
- Sir Tristan: all Units in range, friendly or opposing, lose 1 ATK per Item Tristan's controller controls.
- Sir Gareth: adjacent Characters are protected from individually targeted opposing effects.
- Sir Argavain: exposes the opponent's hand in a public hover-preview panel while face-up.
- Sir Gaheris: returns to hand on reveal unless another friendly Knight with “Sir” in its name is controlled.
- Added Mordred reveal audio metadata and playback for Mordred.mp3 / Mordred 2.mp3.
