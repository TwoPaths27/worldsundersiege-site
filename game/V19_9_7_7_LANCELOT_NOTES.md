# V19.9.7.7 — Sir Lancelot Card Test

- BOA-003 Sir Lancelot now triggers whenever he is played face-up or revealed from Conceal.
- He gains +3 SPD until the end of the current turn. Existing movement spent is preserved, so the bonus adds three usable movement points.
- During that reveal turn, Sir Lancelot may attack battlefield Units and Constructs but cannot attack a Stronghold.
- The restriction ends with the same turn in which he was revealed.
- Reveal presentation plays `../sounds/Lancelot.mp3` at volume 1.0 and `../sounds/Lancelot 2.mp3` at volume 0.6 while background ambience is ducked, then fades ambience back in.
