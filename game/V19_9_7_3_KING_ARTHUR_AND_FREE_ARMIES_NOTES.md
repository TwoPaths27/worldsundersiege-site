# Worlds Under Siege v19.9.7.3

## King Arthur — first finished card pass

Applies to BOA-001, BOA-226, and SD1-001 identities/art variants.

- A face-up recruitment or later reveal triggers King Arthur's presentation audio.
- The ordinary recruitment/placement sound completes first through the existing recruitment flow.
- `../sounds/King Arthur.mp3` (voice, volume 1.0) and `../sounds/King Arthur 2.mp3` (music, volume 0.32) then start together.
- Background ambience fades down before the presentation and fades back to its prior game level afterward.
- Missing or blocked audio never interrupts gameplay or leaves ambience permanently ducked.
- Chosen King is derived continuously from the current battlefield.
- Other friendly Characters, Animals, and Armies gain +2 ATK and +1 SPD.
- King Arthur never buffs himself.
- Concealed, destroyed, absent, or enemy-controlled Arthurs do not grant the aura.
- Multiple separate King Arthur permanents stack naturally if game rules permit them.

## Army energy correction

- Every card with the Army type normalizes to 0 Energy cost.
- Army cards hide the hand cost badge and display `ARMY · FREE`.
- Selecting an Army deploys it directly to the Army Zone without paying Energy.
- Army previews remain cost-free and do not display an Energy cost.
