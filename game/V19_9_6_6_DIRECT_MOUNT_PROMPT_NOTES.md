# V19.9.6.6 — Direct Mount Prompt

- Select a friendly face-up Character.
- Every adjacent, friendly, face-up Animal or Construct with Mount is highlighted.
- Hover or keyboard-focus the legal Mount tile to reveal a centered **MOUNT?** control.
- Activating the control mounts immediately and consumes that Character’s one mount/dismount change for the turn.
- The old preliminary MOUNT button was removed; DISMOUNT remains in the selected-unit controls.
- The prompt is unavailable while the game is over or an animation/action resolution lock is active.
