# V19.9.7.9c — Mounted Item and Tile Glow Fix

- Items can be equipped directly to a mounted Character by clicking the rider half of a mounted tile.
- Mounted tile click interception no longer prevents Item attachment.
- The blue equipped-Item indicator now illuminates the battlefield cell behind the Unit instead of covering the Unit artwork.
- The cell indicator also recognizes Items attached to the rider of a mounted pair.
