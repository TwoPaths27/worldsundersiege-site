Welcome to Worlds Under Siege

Worlds Under Siege is a hybrid strategy game that combines the deck-building depth of a trading card game with the tactical positioning of a board game.

Players construct powerful decks before the match, then deploy their forces onto a shared battlefield where placement, movement, and range are just as important as card effects.

Build your army. Command the battlefield. Besiege the worlds.
